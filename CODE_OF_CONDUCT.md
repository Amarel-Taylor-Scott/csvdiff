# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone and to
act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, and healthy community.
Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, and
graceful acceptance of feedback. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
insults, and publishing others' private information.

Report abuse to **amarel.taylor.s@gmail.com**; reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly. Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), v2.1.
